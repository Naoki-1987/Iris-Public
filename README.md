# Iris Watchface for Pebble 📸

<img width="2752" height="1536" alt="Banner" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/71c0ba7b-c582-4ce3-a90b-0c8d8e871f24" />

Meet **Iris**, a Pebble watch face inspired by the stunning mechanical shutter of a camera. Iris elegantly merges the worlds of analog and digital timekeeping, packing both displays into a single, beautifully animated experience. 

With its smooth shutter animations and unique smart features, Iris is more than just a watch face—it's a sophisticated transformation tool for your Pebble, allowing you to seamlessly switch between a casual and a professional look directly from your wrist, no smartphone required.

## ✨ Key Features
* **Mechanical Shutter Animation:** Beautiful aperture blades that open and close.
* **Business Time Mode:** Automatically transforms to a stealthy, professional analog face during your work hours (integrates with Pebble's Quiet Time).
* **Visual Battery Indicator:** Shutter blades drop one by one as your battery falls below 50%.
* **Smart Periodic Vibrations:** Gentle hourly (or custom) chimes that respect your Quiet Time and Business Time settings.
* **Highly Customizable:** Choose between digital/analog, blade widths, and various color themes including pastel rainbow and solid stealth colors.
